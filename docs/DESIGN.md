# Ironclad: design

An idle tower defence where the tower moves. An armoured train crosses the frontier a mile at a time; raiders ride in
from both sides and along the track; every wagon is a weapon and the order they are coupled in matters. A run ends when
the engine's hull is gone. Steel earned on the way buys workshop upgrades that are kept, so each run gets further.

## A run
- **Miles.** A mile takes 22 seconds. Its raiders arrive over the first 15; the ones still alive carry over.
- **Raiders** strike the nearest car that still has plating. A wagon with no plating stops firing and is passed by until
  a quarter of it is mended. Blows on the engine come off the hull.
- **Scrip** from every raider buys field levels for a car, for this run only: more damage (or more of what a support
  wagon gives) and its plating made good.
- **Every tenth mile** a boss rides. When it is down the train stands at a **station**: one of three cards for the rest
  of the run (Common, Rare, Epic), the plating made good, part of the hull back, and the wagons can be recoupled.
- **The whistle** throws back and stuns every raider near the train (60 s to build steam). Tapping a raider marks it:
  every gun that can reach it fires at it first.
- **The end.** Once a run it can be revived (an advert or gems). The run's steel is banked when it is closed, and can
  be doubled by an advert.

## The train
The engine has its own deck gun. Behind it ride up to eight wagons (two at first): Gatling, Cannon, Supply (neighbours
fire faster), Sharpshooter, Armoured (neighbours take less), Furnace, Mortar, Fitters' (mends plating), Dynamo. Plans
for each turn up at a best mile; steel builds it and raises its level. Support wagons work on the wagons either side of
them, and raiders from behind reach the last wagon first, so order is a real decision.

## Between runs
- **Workshop:** damage, hull, plating, rate of fire, crits, scrip, steel, starting scrip, running repairs, the whistle,
  a longer train, faster running speeds.
- **Seals:** one from every one of the Baron's carriages brought down. They buy seven lasting perks (a higher
  starting field level, knocked-out wagons that come back, an extra card at stations, better card odds, a longer
  whistle stun, more hull on a revive, more depot steel).
- **Lines:** five, each much harder and richer than the last; the next opens at a mile on the one before.
- **Depot:** while the game is closed, patrols bring home a share of the steel the best line would pay, for eight hours.
  One free double a day.

## Money
Free. Rewarded adverts only, never shown unasked: revive, double the ended run's steel, double the depot after the free
one (eight a day). Purchases: four gem packs, Fireman's Pack, Rolling Mill (steel doubled), Conductor's Pass (advert
rewards without the advert), Night Shift (depot +4 h). Nothing in the game needs a purchase.

Not done on purpose: forced adverts, energy, pay-only walls, punishing streaks, fake players.

## Daily and long-term
A seven-day gift (a missed day loses nothing), three daily tasks from a list of six, awards, gems the first time each
station on a line is reached. With AUTO on a finished run collects itself and the train sets off again.
