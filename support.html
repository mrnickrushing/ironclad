Restoring purchases

Open SHOP in the game, scroll to the bottom and tap Restore purchases. One-time purchases (the Fireman's Pack, Rolling Mill, Conductor's Pass and Night Shift) come back on any device signed in with the same Apple Account. Gems and steel travel with your iCloud save; a restore brings back what is kept for good, not gems or steel.

Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your saved game

Your progress is saved on your device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud so it follows you to a new iPhone or iPad. A run in progress is saved too. MORE > Delete account and start over deletes both.

Your account

Signing in with Apple is optional. MORE > Delete account and start over erases everything and signs you out.

Adverts

Ironclad only shows an advert when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching one. Everything in the game can be earned by playing, and the Conductor's Pass gives those rewards without the advert.
